# Vellum Product Roadmap

## Vision

Vellum is an AI-powered Booking Operating System for service businesses.

Our mission is to help service businesses spend less time managing operations and more time serving customers.

Booking is the center of the platform.

Everything else exists to make booking more valuable.

---

# Product Principles

## 1. Booking First

Every feature should improve one of:

- Booking conversion
- Booking experience
- Booking preparation
- Booking completion
- Post-booking follow-up

---

## 2. AI Assists

AI recommends.

AI drafts.

AI summarizes.

AI automates after approval.

AI does not replace business owners.

---

## 3. Automation Removes Busy Work

Every repetitive workflow should eventually become automatable.

---

## 4. One Source of Truth

Business logic belongs in Engines.

Pages render.

Services think.

---

## 5. Beautiful Software Wins

Enterprise software should feel modern.

Simple.

Elegant.

Fast.

Premium.
